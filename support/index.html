Support

Need a hand with Things We See? Here's how to reach us.

Send feedback from TestFlight

If you're testing the app through TestFlight, this is the quickest way to tell us about a problem:

  1. Take a screenshot in the app (press the side button and volume up together).
  2. Tap the screenshot preview in the corner.
  3. Tap Done, then Share Beta Feedback.
  4. Add a short note about what happened and tap Submit.

You can also open the TestFlight app, choose Things We See, and tap Send Beta Feedback.

Contact

Email hello@thingswesee.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Common questions

Where is my data?

On your iPhone. The app has no accounts and no servers. If you use iCloud Backup, your data is also in your own iCloud backup. See the privacy policy.

How do I back up my places and notes?

In the app, go to Profile → Your data → Export with photos, then save the file somewhere safe, such as Files or iCloud Drive. Your regular iCloud Backup also includes the app's data.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app from your iPhone. That removes everything it stored. Any files you exported stay where you saved them.

Is it on the App Store?

Not yet. Things We See is in private testing through TestFlight.
