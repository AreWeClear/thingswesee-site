Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

The short version: Things We See keeps your information on your iPhone. We don't collect it, we can't see it, and we never sell or share it.

This policy covers the Things We See iPhone app, including GottaGo, its travel section.

What we collect

Nothing. Things We See has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools. We don't receive your visits, notes, photos, lists, or location.

What stays on your phone

Your visits, Notes to Self, photos, Wanna Go list, trip plans, and settings are stored only on your iPhone. If you use iCloud Backup, they are included in your own iCloud backup, which is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to it.

Location

The app reads your location only:

  • when you tap I'm here or Pin this place (including asking Siri to pin a place), and
  • if you turn on alerts, through iOS region and visit monitoring, so the app can remind you of a Note to Self or a nearby Wanna Go place. You can turn this off at any time in the app or in iPhone Settings.

Your location is used on your phone and is not sent to us.

Apple Maps

To search for places and estimate drive times, the app uses Apple Maps (MapKit). Those requests go to Apple and are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Other websites

When you open a place's Info, Yelp, Google, or Website page, it opens in an in-app browser. Those sites are run by others and have their own privacy policies.

Sharing and exporting

If you share a list or export your data, the app creates a file on your phone. You decide where it goes, using the iPhone share sheet. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you send it.

Selling or sharing data

We don't sell, rent, or share your information with anyone, because we don't have it.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your phone. Copies in your own iCloud Backup or in files you exported stay under your control.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions? Email hello@thingswesee.com.
