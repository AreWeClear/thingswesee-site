A private companion for the places you go.

Things We See remembers the places you visit and the places you want to try, so the next time you're there, or wondering where to go, it has your back.

  • One tap: "I'm here"Mark a visit in a second. The app finds where you are and keeps the date.
  • Notes to SelfWrite a note about a place ("ask for the patio") and it shows up when you're back.
  • Wanna GoA list of places you want to try, ready when you are.
  • Going outIdeas for right now, from your own places and lists, with drive times.
  • "Pin this place" with SiriDriving past somewhere interesting? Ask Siri to pin it and look later.
  • GottaGo, for tripsThe travel section. Build a destination package for a trip, like a long weekend in Taos.

Everything stays on your phone. No account, no servers, no ads, no tracking. Read the privacy policy.

Availability

Things We See is an iPhone app in private testing through Apple's TestFlight. It isn't on the App Store yet.
